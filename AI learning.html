1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

AI is the simulation of human intelligence by machines, specifically computer systems.

2. Who created AI?

John McCarthy is widely recognized as the father of AI, coining the term in 1955.

3. When was it announced?

The field of AI research was born at the Dartmouth Workshop in the summer of 1956.

4. How does AI impact students' education, lifestyle, and skills?

AI acts as both a powerful assistant and a challenge. It changes how we search for information and create content.
